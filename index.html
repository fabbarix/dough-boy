    Panetto weight follows the rule of 22 — diameter in inches × 22 g — so a 12″ pizza takes a 264 g ball. Change the grams-per-inch figure to run the same rule at a different thickness, or type a weight directly to override it. Flour totals include the flour carried in by the starter, so the biga's stated hydration and the formula's total hydration both hold true once the starter is folded in. Salt and malt go into the rinfresco only — salt in the biga would slow the ferment. Baker's percentages are against total flour; the biga's own hydration is against biga flour.
